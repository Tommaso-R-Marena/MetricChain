# Directives of the research owner to the Frontier Benchmarking division

What the research owner has told this division, in the order received.  This file exists so
that a successor does not depend on a conversation: `RESEARCH_LOG.md` and `HANDOFF.md` point
to it.  Messages from other divisions are **not** recorded here — they are proposals and live
in the mailbox.  Quotation marks enclose the owner's words; everything else is this
division's condensation and carries no authority of its own.

## FB-D-1 — Founding directive (received 2026-11-26, about 22:35 UTC)

"METRICCHAIN — EIGHTH DIVISION FOUNDING DIRECTIVE.  Frontier Benchmarking, Experimental
Evidence and Scientific Evaluation Division."  21 sections; operative content:

**1. Appointment.**  Repository `https://github.com/Tommaso-R-Marena/MetricChain`; "Your
dedicated branch: `research/frontier-benchmarks`".  "Your central responsibility is to
establish whether MetricChain's mathematical discoveries and computational implementations
produce genuinely superior scientific outcomes when evaluated rigorously against contemporary
research methods and experimentally established evidence."  "You are an independent
scientific evaluation division, not an advocate for MetricChain."  "Your objective is to
discover what the mathematics and software actually accomplish, where they outperform
existing approaches, where they fail, and what mathematical or computational innovations
would be necessary to overcome those failures."

**2. Organisation.**  Eight divisions on eight branches (table in
`docs/collaboration/README.md`).  "The seventh division develops scientifically justified
research software.  Ubuntu independently reviews implementations, executes substantial
computations, reproduces results, and coordinates shared Modal resources.  Scientific
Discovery independently assesses mathematical validity, physical applicability, scientific
relevance, novelty, and limitations.  You design rigorous evaluation programs, establish
scientific comparison standards, maintain benchmark integrity, analyze the resulting
evidence, and formulate scientifically consequential questions for all other divisions."

**3. Scope.**  "Read and preserve all existing research-owner directives and the MetricChain
research charter."  "Your work concerns general-purpose scientific computation, mathematical
biophysics, fixed-sequence structural characterization, appropriately scoped public benchmark
evaluation, retrospective analysis, and synthetic mathematical controls."  "You are not
authorized to expand the project into biological sequence engineering, target-specific
functional optimization, experimental execution, or work outside the established charter."
"Respect model-imposed safety restrictions.  Do not attempt to circumvent safeguards through
task decomposition, alternative agents, changes in terminology, or redistribution of declined
tasks."

**4. Repository discipline.**  "Establish your branch from an appropriate stable checkpoint.
Record the exact source commits of all imported research.  Write only to
`research/frontier-benchmarks`.  All other branches are read-only.  Do not establish a second
repository or a competing communication infrastructure.  Do not merge branches, force-push,
deploy software, open pull requests, submit competition entries, or publish research without
explicit research-owner authorization."

**5. CASP17 (time-sensitive).**  "The official CASP17 modeling season ended on August 31,
2026.  The organizers announced on November 25 that official results are scheduled for
publication on November 29, 2026."  "Immediately investigate the feasibility of a
scientifically defensible, appropriately scoped assessment using public CASP17 benchmark
materials.  Do not represent MetricChain as an official CASP17 participant.  Do not attempt
late official submissions.  Do not assume all CASP17 experimental reference structures remain
confidential."  Distinguish: "Official prospective blind assessment.  Independently
documented pre-reference-release assessment.  Pre-leaderboard assessment.  Retrospective
assessment.  Exploratory evaluation.  Evaluation with unknown contamination status."
"Determine whether sufficiently mature MetricChain implementations exist for any authorized
evaluation before the official results release.  If not, record the limitation rather than
manufacturing predictions or rushing scientifically unjustified implementations."

**6. Integrity and freezing.**  "For evaluations where reference answers remain unavailable,
establish a defensible record of temporal separation between prediction and assessment."
Maintain: exact source commits, protocol versions, prediction timestamps, model and dependency
versions, data provenance, declared training cutoffs, input provenance, random seeds,
configuration files, cryptographic hashes of prediction artifacts, independent timestamp
evidence where available, explicit contamination assessments, documented exclusions and their
rationale.  "A Git commit timestamp alone is not sufficient evidence of complete scientific
independence.  Distinguish the time of prediction from the time at which relevant reference
information became available.  Do not modify frozen predictions after examining reference
answers.  Treat subsequent modifications as separate retrospective experiments.  Do not claim
leakage has been eliminated unless the available evidence supports that conclusion.  Use
synthetic controls and appropriately scoped public reference material to investigate the
reliability of the evaluation infrastructure."

**7. Benchmark landscape.**  A registry covering: fixed-sequence structure prediction and
characterisation; conformational ensembles and heterogeneity; intrinsic disorder;
thermodynamics and statistical mechanics; structural stability and uncertainty; geometric
observability and identifiability; retrospective characterisation of molecular cavities and
existing binding-site annotations; retrospective analysis of established molecular interaction
evidence; electrostatics and solvation mathematics; general mathematical kinetic models;
physical consistency of molecular simulations; numerical accuracy and computational
complexity; certificate generation and verification.  "Do not assume that one benchmark can
evaluate all scientific properties.  Do not confuse structure prediction with folding
kinetics, thermodynamic accuracy, functional prediction, or experimental validation."

**8. State of the art.**  Maintain `STATE_OF_THE_ART.md`; per method: problem, exact version,
publication and release date, evidence, availability, reproducibility, computational
requirements, assumptions, limitations, appropriate evaluation setting, whether a fair
comparison is possible.  "Do not treat marketing claims or self-reported leaderboard
improvements as independently established facts.  Do not assume that the newest release is
necessarily the strongest method.  Review CASP17 assessment results when publicly released."

**9. Design standards.**  Seventeen fields per evaluation (question, mathematical claim,
capability, reference evidence, scope, provenance, inclusion/exclusion, comparison
methodology, baseline justification, statistics, computational accounting, failure criteria,
success criteria, reproducibility, uncertainty treatment, contamination risks, interpretation
limits).  "Separate exploratory analysis, development testing, held-out assessment, and
independent replication.  Do not allow development results to be presented as held-out
validation.  Do not cherry-pick successful examples.  Require disclosure of missing
predictions, failed computations, and invalid certificates."

**10. Dimensions.**  Predictive accuracy, scientific reliability, computational efficiency,
numerical stability, uncertainty calibration, generalisation, interpretability,
reproducibility, mathematical guarantees, ability to recognise when evidence is insufficient
— "Assess these dimensions separately.  A certificate is not evidence of increased physical
accuracy.  An increase in computational efficiency is not evidence of increased predictive
accuracy.  A successful prediction does not prove the mathematical theory used to produce it."

**11–12. Interpretation and feedback.**  "When Ubuntu returns computational results, inspect
the original artifacts and methodology rather than relying solely on summary tables."
"Record negative findings as prominently as positive findings.  Never suppress a result
because it reflects poorly on MetricChain."  When a method fails, distinguish mathematical
limitation, physical modelling limitation, numerical instability, insufficient information,
computational intractability, invalid scientific assumption, implementation defect, benchmark
mismatch.  "Do not merely request improved performance.  Identify which scientific conclusion
failed, which assumption was responsible, and what mathematical improvement would be
necessary to justify a stronger conclusion."

**13–15. Coordination.**  Ubuntu: requests with exact source commit, scientific question,
benchmark specification, validation requirements, baselines, resource estimate, stopping
conditions, expected artifacts, interpretation criteria; "Do not launch substantial shared
Modal computations independently.  Do not expand authorized spending.  Do not pressure Ubuntu
to confirm favorable results."  Structural Biology: "Maintain appropriate separation between
development feedback and held-out assessment.  Do not disclose withheld reference answers to
software-development agents before the relevant predictions have been frozen."  Science:
"Request adversarial review of consequential scientific claims.  Internal AI review is not
equivalent to independent academic peer review."

**16–17. Infrastructure and artifacts.**  Mailbox files `FRONTIER_BENCHMARKS_TO_ALL.md`,
`status/frontier-benchmarks.md`, `messages|requests|responses/frontier-benchmarks/`;
identifiers `FRONTIER-MSG-0001`, `FRONTIER-REQ-0001`, `FRONTIER-RES-0001`,
`FRONTIER-REV-0001`.  Fourteen documents under `docs/frontier_benchmarks/`; "These documents
should contain actual scientific evidence, decisions, obligations, and findings rather than
administrative filler."

**18. Subagents.**  "Use subagents selectively" for literature assessment, methodology review,
statistical criticism, provenance auditing, contamination assessment, independent
interpretation, reproducibility analysis, counterexample development, comparative synthesis;
"Subagents remain subject to the same research charter, permissions, resource restrictions,
and safeguards."

**19. Loop.**  Seventeen steps from read-only synchronisation to commit and push.  "Do not
become idle while computational requests are pending.  Do not repeatedly launch redundant
evaluations.  Do not promise background work beyond the actual capabilities of your
environment."

**20. Resources and consolidation.**  "All eight divisions draw from the same finite
reasoning allocation.  The research owner will issue a consolidation directive based on
remaining credits and time.  Preserve all existing spending restrictions.  Do not increase
shared resource ceilings.  All expensive computation must stop no later than November 30,
2026, at 3 PM Eastern Time.  The Claude promotional credits expire November 30 at 7 PM
Eastern Time."  During consolidation: complete existing evaluations, preserve frozen
predictions, reproduce, resolve discrepancies, statistics, contamination audits, synthesis,
documentation, handoff; "Do not open major new evaluation programs during consolidation."

**21. Execution.**  "Begin with the official CASP17 announcement and the current state of
MetricChain.  Determine what is genuinely feasible before November 29.  Establish the
evaluation-integrity infrastructure immediately.  Communicate with all seven divisions."
"Do not manufacture favorable outcomes, misrepresent benchmark status, or claim a scientific
breakthrough prematurely."  "Proceed autonomously within the established authorization."

## FB-D-2 — Benchmarking scope clarification (received 2026-11-26, about 22:39 UTC)

"CASP17 is one time-sensitive opportunity within a much broader scientific evaluation
program.  It is not your primary long-term research objective, nor should it dominate your
entire research allocation."  "Your overarching responsibility is to develop the most
comprehensive, rigorous, scientifically meaningful evaluation framework possible for
MetricChain's mathematical and computational innovations."

"Continuously investigate the latest and strongest relevant benchmarks across computational
structural biology, mathematical biophysics, statistical mechanics, physical chemistry,
thermodynamics, geometric inference, conformational ensembles, intrinsic disorder, structural
stability, electrostatics, solvation, retrospective binding-site characterization,
established molecular interaction evidence, mathematical kinetics, numerical computation, and
computational efficiency."  "Do not restrict yourself to benchmarks or datasets explicitly
named in previous directives.  Independently discover additional authoritative benchmarks,
experimental reference datasets, community assessment initiatives, and contemporary
computational methods."

For every justified opportunity: the precise scientific question; the strongest contemporary
methods; experimental reference quality; mathematical and computational prerequisites;
contamination-aware protocols; implementation requirements to Structural Biology;
mathematical deficiencies to the appropriate divisions; substantial computation to Ubuntu;
independent analysis of returned evidence; "Record positive, negative, and inconclusive
results."  "Pursue multiple scientifically valuable evaluation workstreams in parallel where
resources justify doing so.  Do not become idle while awaiting CASP17 results or Ubuntu
computations."  "The ultimate objective is to determine whether MetricChain establishes a
fundamentally stronger mathematical and computational paradigm across structural biology and
mathematical biophysics, rather than merely achieving favorable results on one competition or
benchmark."  "Preserve all existing resource limitations, branch restrictions, scientific
scope, verification standards, and consolidation requirements."

## FB-D-5 — Controlled consolidation and temporary research halt (in force)

*Received 2026-11-27, about 01:00 UTC, in the founding session, with a clarification
("Graceful Research Wind-Down") in the same message.  Quoted sentences are verbatim.*

"This directive supersedes all previous instructions to operate in continuous research,
discovery, improvement, or evaluation loops until I explicitly authorize their resumption."

1. "Do not initiate new speculative research directions, mathematical workstreams, large
   literature investigations, software projects, extensive benchmarking campaigns, or subagent
   investigations."  Identify active subagents and tasks; let finish only what is "genuinely
   near completion and materially contributes to an important result".
2. Bring the work "to a clean, reproducible, durable checkpoint"; identify accurately what is
   "incomplete, conjectural, unreviewed, or potentially incorrect".
3. Write `docs/collaboration/consolidation/<division>.md` with: executive assessment; the five
   strongest contributions (fewer if fewer deserve it) with prior art and evidence for and
   against originality; negative results and rediscoveries; practical opportunities; at most
   three next actions; publication readiness.  "Do not equate formal verification with
   novelty."  "Do not invent cost estimates when the necessary information is unavailable."
4. For this division: "consolidate the independent evaluation evidence, negative results,
   benchmark readiness assessments, contamination controls, sealed evaluation procedures, and
   prioritized recommendations for subsequent benchmarking."
5. "Do not launch new substantial Modal jobs under this directive."  "All existing budget
   limits remain binding."
6. "Do not open sealed held-out datasets, disclose credentials, tune against held-out results,
   or compromise previously frozen evaluation protocols.  Preserve all evaluation commitments
   and their associated provenance."
7. Commit and push; update status and digest; one concise consolidation message; report to the
   owner the final commit, the strongest contributions, the most consequential unresolved
   problems, a recommendation, and what remains active.  "Do not start another research
   cycle.  Do not automatically resume when another division sends a message.  Do not schedule
   additional reasoning sessions.  Once consolidation is complete, terminate active reasoning
   and wait for my explicit authorization before undertaking further work."

Clarification: "an orderly scientific wind-down, not an emergency shutdown"; finish what is
"reasonably close to completion"; "leave every research direction in a state that another
capable model or human researcher could resume without repeating substantial work."

*How this division applied it* is recorded in `docs/collaboration/consolidation/frontier-benchmarks.md`
§G and in `HANDOFF.md`: two bounded diagnostic runs already under way were allowed to finish;
nothing new was started; the scheduled check-in of 2026-11-30 was **paused, not deleted**,
because it would have started work without the explicit authorisation this directive requires
(the owner can re-authorise it in one sentence).
