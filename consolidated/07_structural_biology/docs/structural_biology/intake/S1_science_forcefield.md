## 0. Decision-relevant summary

1. **No real protein structure has been touched.** Every "real force field" result is on synthetic capped peptides (`Ace-Xₙ-NMe`) built from ideal internal coordinates on uniform ideal backbones, with a three-state grid per side-chain dihedral. In the division's words: "no structure file, no rotamer library from a database, no sequence varied or optimised" (`SCI-RES-0012` §4; `SCI-MSG-0025` §8). What is real is the energy function: OpenMM 8.6.1's ff14SB and GBn2.
2. **What exists is a research pipeline for certified brackets of `ln Z` of ff14SB + GBn2 rotamer models on chains**, up to 20–30 residues, with widths of `10⁻²`–`10⁻⁴` nats. Its logical skeleton is machine-checked; its constants, tables, evaluator and arithmetic are not; the code is unreviewed research code; no self-contained free-energy certificate file exists.
3. **GMEC-with-certificate on a pairwise energy over public structures is not something this division has done.** Its own benchmarks B2 (certificate cost on public side-chain networks) and B5 (is the certified minimum meaningful) are recorded as "nothing yet" (`BENCHMARK_DESIGN.md` §4). STRUCTBIO's leading candidate would not duplicate it.
4. **The tractable model class is a band along the sequence of a chain** (transfer matrix). Folded proteins are not bands; tree decompositions are an open obligation (`G-3`). This is the main obstacle to porting the free-energy line to PDB structures **(my inference)**.

## A. What was actually built on real force fields

**A.1 Force field, solvent, software** (`SP_REPORT.md` §3.1; `P2_REPORT.md` §3.1; `P3_REPORT.md` §2.0). Parameter files shipped with OpenMM 8.6.1: `amber14/protein.ff14SB.xml` (known part); `implicit/gbn2.xml` = `GBSAGBn2Force` (Amber `igb=8`, mbondi3 radii, `ε_in = 1`, `ε_out = 78.5`, no salt) with `sasaMethod=None`, i.e. **polarisation energy only, no surface-area term**; no cutoff, no constraints; 300 K, `k_BT = 0.596161 kcal/mol`. For comparison residuals the pilot also used `implicit/obc2.xml`, `amber99sb.xml`, `amber19/protein.ff19SB.xml`, `amber03.xml`, `amber14/protein.ff15ipq.xml`. **A.3 Discrete state space.** Grid R3: every side-chain dihedral ∈ {−60°, 180°, +60°}; Val 3, Leu/Phe/Ser 9 (Ser: χ₁ and the hydroxyl torsion), Glu 27, Lys 81 states before filtering. Clash filter: `min r_pq/(R_p + R_q) > τ` over atom pairs ≥ 4 bonds apart, `τ = 0.70` (0.60 "relaxed"). Admissible states per interior site at 0.70/0.60: Ser 9/9, Val 3/3, Leu 0/2 (**leucine has no admissible state at 0.70**; all Leu runs use 0.60, two states), Phe 2/5–7, Lys 21/31. Where two rotamers can clash (Phe, Lys, LKSE) the clash-free domain is not a product and the theorems were applied on a **greedily pruned sub-product `X′`**; in 14 of 40 such analyses the minimum of the clash-free domain is not in `X′` (`DEAD_ENDS.md` SD45; head notes of `P2_REPORT.md`, `P3_REPORT.md`). Sizes reached: enumeration to 923 521 states; beyond it Val₂₀/PPII `3.49·10⁹`, Val₃₀ `10^14.3`, Leu₃₀/H `1.07·10⁹`, Ser₁₂/H `4.9·10⁹`, Ser₂₀ `8·10¹⁵`, Lys₁₂/H `9.5·10¹⁰`, Lys₂₀ `5.5·10¹⁷`.

**A.4 Energy decomposition.** `E = f + g`. *Known part* `f` = ff14SB vacuum energy (torsions + Lennard-Jones + Coulomb), **exactly pairwise in residue-rotamer variables**: `f = c₀ + Σ h_i(x_i) + Σ J_ij(x_i, x_j)` to `2·10⁻¹²` kcal/mol in 217 analyses (P2 Statement 1 [P; checked numerically]); not pairwise with one site per χ. *Residual* `g` = GBn2 polarisation energy, many-body through the Born radii. Anchored expansion about a reference assignment `x⁰`: `g = I_{m−1}g + R`, where `I_{m−1}g` needs `g` on the Hamming ball of radius `m − 1` (`N_ball = Σ_{j<m} C(n,j)(k−1)^j` evaluations) and `|R(x)| ≤ Σ_{T ⊆ S(x), |T| = m} ĉ_T`, `S(x)` the sites off the anchor (P3 Proposition 1, Theorem 3).

**A.5 Measured versus certified constants.**
* *Measured* (exact maxima over enumerated finite grids — "a lower bound for the continuous conformation space and not an upper bound for anything"): first/second/third-order class constants `c_i`, `c_ij`, `c_ijk` (P, P2). Rotamer variables: `max c_i` Val 0.4–0.8, Leu 1.0–1.6, Phe 1.6–4.5, Ser 5.0–9.4 kcal/mol (saturating); poly-Lys 10, 37, 52, 56 for `n = 1…4`, ≥ 138 at 24 (growing); `c_ijk` 0.003–0.2 for neutral side chains, up to 2.4 for charged.
* *Certified* ("paper proofs checked against enumeration, interval arithmetic for every derivative bound, a stated floating-point allowance; **not machine-checked, code not reviewed**", `PHYSICAL_APPLICABILITY.md` §2b): P3's term-by-term constants `ĉ_T`, `Γ_m`, `γ_i` (interval enclosures by `mpmath.iv`, `ws_P3/encl.py`); P8's state-wise constants (Proposition S, Theorem AH); P9's state-resolved tables `Θ_T(x_T)` (Theorem R, Proposition P).
* *The gap*: analysis alone gives constants 33–1 400 times the measured ones; the certified tail bound is 250–800 times the true remainder (P3); P9's resolved envelope still exceeds the remainder by ≥ 10.8–17 at every state of the enumerated instances, median 312–449 (`ASSUMPTION_AUDIT.md` A10).

**A.6 The twenty-residue bracket — exact statement** (`P3_REPORT.md` §3.5; `SCI-MSG-0020`, commit `a841cb4181be09ad858eaa36127331d278c53cd4`). Instance: Ace-Val₂₀-NMe, PPII backbone, grid R3, `τ = 0.70`, 332 atoms, 20 sites × 3 rotamers, `|X| = 3²⁰ = 3 486 784 401`. * *Machine-checked* [V]: the remainder identity at every order, the allocation lemma, the sandwich — twice (`experiments/science/ws_L4/RemainderIdentity.lean`: `remainder_identity`, `allocation_lemma`, `anchored_sandwich`, `anchored_sandwich_shares`; theory division's `AnchoredRemainder.lean` at `9408e33`). "Not formal: the constants (P3's enclosures) and the banded models."

* *Later tightening* (P6, `c1b710cf…`): pair shares at `m = 5`, 0.0162 nats; triple shares at `m = 4`, `[518.2383, 518.2929]` from 9 921 evaluations.

**A.7 Vocabulary, exactly.**
* **Sandwich certificate** (P3 Theorem 5; checker specification `SCI-MSG-0025` §8, `P6_REPORT.md` §7): data = instance tables, anchor, ball values of `g`, penalty numbers, band; claim `m₋ ≤ E ≤ m₊` ⇒ `ln Z(m₊) ≤ ln Z(E) ≤ ln Z(m₋)`, both ends by exact transfer matrix. A specified object, **not a file format**: the scripts write JSON run summaries and regenerable `.npz` caches only (my reading of the code; a "format note" for the recovery is still owed to engineering, `SCI-RES-0022`).
* **Shares** (P6 Lemmas A, A′; Proposition P6.1): how each tail constant `ĉ_T` is charged — to sites (`t/m`), pairs (`C(t,2)/C(m,2)`) or triples of `T`; pointwise tighter, models stay exactly solvable. Machine-checked by theory (`ShareLevels.lean`, `f7df44f`).
* **Certified populations** (addendum A17 (13)): `P_E(A) ∈ [Z₊(A)/(Z₊(A)+Z₋(Aᶜ)), Z₋(A)/(Z₋(A)+Z₊(Aᶜ))]`, sharp for the sandwich; all 60 single-site rotamer populations of Val₂₀ within a relative 1.2 %.
* **Shares at the leaves** (P7; `SCI-RES-0014`): a certificate that `x₀` **minimises a pairwise lower model** — a branching tree whose leaves carry LP-dual messages; it is exactly the theory division's `Cert` (`branch`, `leaf`; `proofs/CertificateAlgebra.lean`, `verify_sound` [V]). JSON files with integers in units of `2⁻⁴⁰` kcal/mol, tables rounded down; checking is "only additions and comparisons of integers". Nine certificates accepted by the workstream's checker, by `verify` under `#eval`, by the coordinator's independent checker and by engineering's compiled checker. This is the division's only GMEC-type artefact; its instances rest on *measured* class constants.
* **First-order recovery** (Proposition A17.19; `pi_inference/slack_recovery.py`): the slack that the band gives up is a known table; Gibbs–Bogoliubov with exact means under the ceiling (held-site transfer-matrix passes) takes it out of the bracket. Lys₁₂/H, order-4 ball (96 366 evaluations), band 7: 0.0073 → `[517.1285101, 517.1285931]`, `8.3·10⁻⁵` nats. Finite form machine-checked by theory (`GibbsRecovery.lean`: `lower_jensen`, `lower_product`, `upper_jensen`, `upper_product`, `3cf14b9`).
* **Second-order recovery — "Lys₁₂ within `2.1·10⁻⁴`"** (`SCI-RES-0035`, commit `59e88bc895e0144f16b6f3d2278978ae29afb25c`; Proposition A17.23; `pi_inference/held_pair_exact.py`): with *two* held sites, pair terms are differences of clamped partition functions; a second Bonferroni bound under the floor gives an upper end with no change of measure. Result: Lys₁₂, order 4, **band 4**: `[517.1284375, 517.1286523]`, **0.0002148 nats instead of 0.0006449**. It is not the division's narrowest Lys₁₂ bracket (band 7, first order, is `8.2·10⁻⁵`); it shows that the "loss between groups" is recoverable. Status: [P], "has been read by nobody else"; validated against enumeration on four five-residue instances (exact `ln Z` inside); one twelve-residue run; double precision with a `10⁻⁹` margin per term; inherits P9's tables. Part (24) of the addenda is unrefereed.

What a "certified number" is, in the division's adopted words (addendum A17 (21), item 83 (f)): an interval for `ln Z` of one model energy "**given** that workstream P9's tables satisfy its Theorem R at every state (a paper proof by one workstream, machine-checked in its abstract skeleton only, tested where the tables exceed the remainder at least 8.9-fold …); **given** the evaluation tolerance … (assumed); with one implementation of the evaluator and of the tables; in double precision with margins of `10⁻⁹` and no interval control. It is not a statement about a peptide in water, not a machine-checked certificate".

**A.8 Where it fails, and why.**
* *Backbone-torsion variables*: no pairwise known part (`c_ij(f)` up to 28 kcal/mol); GBn2 constants `βc = 12–53`, growing with length; no sharp site (SD19).
* *Analysis without evaluations*: loses a factor 33–1 400 because a term-by-term bound cannot see the cancellation of self and cross terms (Val₆: −640 + 600 = −37 kcal/mol). P5 makes this a theorem: Born radii do not depend on charges, so every bound using charges only through `|q|` holds for all sign patterns and exceeds the truth by 16–153 at orders 3–5 on leucine helices (SD47).
* *Helix (compact)*: at checkpoint 40 "fails at every affordable order" (Leu₁₆: `Γ₃…Γ₆ = 1 819, 370, 60, 8.0` kcal/mol) — many neighbours within the range of the kernel, many-body terms decaying like `r⁻⁴`. Later certified where the ensemble stays near its anchor (Leu₃₀, order 6: "this helix ensemble is nearly frozen") and, with exact window terms, for Ser₁₂ at order 6 (0.012 nats).
* *Charged side chains*: an unscreened charge–charge reaction-field term stays in the residual (SD37); P3's constants gave `βΓ_m = 689, 145, 23.4` for Lys₁₂ at orders 4–6. Rescued at twelve residues by P8/P9 plus recovery; **(LKSE)₃ at order 3 has no certificate (658.9 nats)**: the floor "delocalises", and "when a floor delocalises is not predicted by anything we have" (P9: "wrong five times of nine"). Lys₂₀ (`[800.5150660, 800.5154710]`) "has been compared with nothing".
* *Never measured*: a buried environment — "there the third-order constants must be expected to be larger" (request P2-E1, not decided).

## C. The division's physical-applicability verdicts

`PHYSICAL_APPLICABILITY.md` §0: "**The mathematics is about models, never about molecules.** … none of the constants the theory needs has ever been *certified* for a production force field, except for corrections with an explicit low-dimensional form (torsion refits)."

| object | status on real force fields | where |
|---|---|---|
| known part, pairwise | exact on a fixed backbone with one site per residue; does not exist in backbone torsions | §1; P2 Statement 1 |
| first-order (Lipschitz) constants `c_i` | measured only; same-lineage refits exactly certifiable (ff99SB − ff14SB: range 1.115 kcal/mol on the circle); GBn2: `βc = 12–53` in torsions; saturate for neutral rotamers, grow for charged | §2, §2a |
| second-order `c_ij` | measured; star certificate exact for apolar side chains (25 of 531 441 evaluations), fails for polar/charged because the *pair* part is large | §2a |
| third/higher-order | measured small (0.003–0.2 kcal/mol, neutral); certified only with loss 33–1 400 (analysis) or by ball + tail | §2a, §2b |
| floors/ceilings | "the certificate must be a function, not a constant"; certified on extended backbones; a floor can delocalise | §2b; audit A8 |
| `L²`/RMS bounds | "certifies nothing": `0.1 k_BT` RMS allows an undetected `100 k_BT` dip on a state of probability `10⁻⁶` | §7 |

*Vacuous guarantees*: per-torsion class at 10 residues — uncertifiable width 430–480 nats, ≥ `10⁵` evaluations; "the class is expensive, not the function" (Jensen gap 2–3 nats against 71–178 allowed). Analysis-only floors: cost numbers 16 to `10⁵` (valine, strand) and `10²–10⁸⁹` (helices, charged side chains). Delocalised floors: 187 nats (Leu₁₂, order 4), 658.9 nats.

*Model error against certified error*: helix, β and PPII lie within 0.4–2.7 kcal/mol and "a torsion refit of 0.64 kcal/mol per residue reorders them"; charge models differ by 1.4–5 kcal/mol; "the accuracy that matters is about `1.7 k_BT` on differences". Against certified widths of `10⁻⁴` nats the model error is larger by four or more orders **(my inference from their numbers)**. The vacuum force field is a worse model of the solvated serine ensemble than fitted independent residues (`D_2 = 4–45` against 0.0015–0.096; B10).

*What a meaningful question is* (§1, §5, §6; `SP_REPORT.md` §2.B): `min E` "predicts a structure only if its basin dominates and the model error is below the gap to competing basins"; `ln Z` is "**observable** only in differences"; under model uncertainty the meaningful object is the set of *possibly optimal* conformations (B5). "today a certificate with certified inputs exists only where there is no residual, or where the residual has an explicit analytic form" — the first named candidate being "Fixed-backbone side-chain placement with a pairwise force field and no residual", where "the comparison to make is with existing exact solvers" (that literature cited "[K, from memory — unverified]"). §6 forbids claiming "that a certified minimum is a predicted structure" and "that anything here has been compared with a measurement. Nothing has."

## D. BENCHMARK_DESIGN

"A design, not a result." Principles (§0): every benchmark attached to a hypothesis it could refute; planted truth for anything claiming certainty ("one miss … is a bug, not a statistic"); negative controls and deception instances; "baselines are the methods a practitioner would use, run by their own published recipes"; development/evaluation separation with seeds and metrics fixed in advance; what success would not establish written next to each benchmark; "fixed sequences and synthetic or public data only; no sequence design, no target-specific optimisation, no experiments."

* **B1** (which class): capped peptides 2–12 residues, "later a small folded domain with a fixed backbone and rotamers". Pilot and rotamer versions run; buried case open.
* **B2** (certificate cost): "public fixed-sequence side-chain-positioning networks (engineering's E-2), stratified by treewidth and by the number of sites whose gap is below a stated model uncertainty"; routes: native Lean-kernel certificate, the same tree as a VeriPB proof checked by CakePB (and PBLean), the solver without proof; H2 refuted if the pseudo-Boolean route is within a factor three; mutation tests. **Not run.**
* **B5** (meaning of the minimum): sharp sites, contact-set size, robustly certified fraction. **Not run.**
* **B10**: run in full by the coordinator on P2's instances.

No dataset is named. By my search, "PDB" occurs nowhere in `docs/science/`, the science messages or `experiments/science/`, and data or software licences are not discussed anywhere there; toulbar2, OSPREY, Rosetta, SCWRL and FASPR are not named in this file (its named baselines are VeriPB/CakePB/PBLean, maximum-entropy reweighting, maximum occurrence, plain sampling). Related prior art (workstream A2, `SCI-REV-0006`, their reading levels): "no proof logging for cost-function networks / weighted CSP / MAP found: toulbar2 1.0.0 guide and repository"; verified pseudo-Boolean proof checking "within a factor 20 of solving time" (cited as Koops et al., CP 2025); "'provable' in the OSPREY literature means a paper proof relative to the model, floating point not discussed".
