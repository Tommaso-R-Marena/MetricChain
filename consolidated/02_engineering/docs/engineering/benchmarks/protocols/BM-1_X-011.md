# Protocol of X-011 (campaign BM-1) — written before jobs M-016 and M-017 were launched

Committed before the launch; not edited afterwards except for the section "Deviations"
at the end.  Registry entries Q-01, Q-02, Q-03.

## What is evaluated

Nested runs of Algorithm 2L⁺ on the cube class `L_u(Q_n)` (theory report §11.19; the text of
`K_REPORT.md` §§2–3 at theory `63efc69`), with every rung of every level served by the
candidate of smallest **certified** cost (remark (1b) of §11.19 at theory `dd067f1`;
THY-RES-0013 §4; reviewed in SCI-REV-0016).  Top level as in ENG-RES-0014 §2: `b = 0.5`,
`λ = 0.2`, `η = η′ = θ = 0.05`, `δ = 0.05`, ladder `x_l = x₀(b/x₀)^{l/40}`, certified accuracy
`0.8715`.  `u = 1` in this stage.  Unit: `ln(evaluations)/(n a)`, `a = KL(p_u ‖ ½) = 0.11094`.

* **M-016 (bounds).**  `B_k(n)`: the least certified bound with at most `k` nested levels of
  Algorithm 2L⁺ above base brackets (`k + 1` "levels" in the reports), `k = 1 … 5`, over 18
  outer fractions of the top level and one of two candidate families for the levels
  below it ("theory", "wide": `src/metricchain_eng/twolevel/certified.py`).  An exact
  minimisation over the family; no instance.
* **M-017 (runs).**  The design selected at each of ten outer fractions (family "theory",
  `k = 1, 2, 3`), run in the mean-run model at the flat instance and on the worst outer
  landscape a search finds, fibres flat, or cones with the base brackets' own answers
  (reading (B)), or cones with base brackets at an end of their promise (reading (A)).
  Comparator: the referee's design, whose servers were chosen at the flat fibre
  (ENG-RES-0014, table `three_worst`).  Also the two-level worst cases the theory
  division asked for (R16-THY-1(b)).

## Hypotheses, stated before the run

* **H1 (saturation).**  `B_k(n)` is nonincreasing in `k` (true by construction — a test of the
  implementation) and stops decreasing at some `k*(n) ≤ 5` for every `n ≤ 128 000`; nesting
  does not pay at `n = 1000` (`k* = 1`); `k*(4000) = 3` (local runs before launch: `0.7262,
  0.6106, 0.5936, 0.5935` for `k = 1 … 4`).  *Refuted if* a fifth nested level still lowers
  the bound by more than `0.001` at some size.
* **H2 (distance from the lower bound).**  The excess of the best certified bound over the
  lower bound for every algorithm, `[B_{k*}(n) − LB(n)]·n a` in nats, **grows** with `n` over
  the whole range — the certified designs do not approach `e^{nh}` in absolute terms at
  accessible sizes — while the ratio `B_{k*}(n)/LB(n)` decreases.  Theorem 11.58a predicts an
  excess `O(√(n ln n))` asymptotically with a constant of order `8.6` for its own design;
  expectation here: an effective constant `excess/√(n ln n)` near `1` and far below `8.6`
  (`0.9` at `n = 16 000` from local runs).  No prediction of its trend; the fit of
  `excess = c·n^γ` is reported whatever it gives.
* **H3 (the family matters little).**  "wide" lowers `B_k(n)` by less than `0.02` against
  "theory" for `k ≤ 3` at every size (local: `0` at `k = 2`, `0.007` at `k = 3`, `n = 4000`).
* **H4 (tuned against the worst case or at the flat instance — the research owner's
  question).**  For three levels (`k = 2`) at `n = 4000` and `16 000`: (a) the worst instance
  found for the certified-cost design, minimised over the outer fraction, is **below** the
  referee's flat-tuned design's (`0.6249`, `0.5525` in reading (B); `0.6826` in reading (A) at
  `n = 4000`); (b) its cost at the flat instance is **above** the flat-tuned design's
  (`0.4530`, `0.3893`).  *Refuted if* (a) fails at either size in reading (B).
* **H5 (consistency).**  Every searched cost lies between the cost at the flat instance
  and the certified bound of its design, with no violated claim of Theorem 11.56 in any
  evaluated run.  A cost above the bound would contradict the theorem's cost bound in
  the mean-run model and is reported as a finding with its instance.
* **H6 (tightness).**  How far the worst instance found stays below the certified bound:
  no prediction.  Reported per size and number of levels.
* **H7 (R16-THY-1(b), the theory division's expectation).**  Two-level worst cases at
  `n = 8000, 32 000, 64 000` within `0.006` of `0.619`, `0.550`, `0.537` (ENG-RES-0008, other
  ladder), hence above the certified three-level bounds `0.5262`, `0.4439`, `0.4341`.

## Analysis fixed in advance

Tables by `n` and `k` of: the bound; the outer fraction that attains it; the cost at the
flat instance of the selected design; worst found per reading, per fraction and
minimised over the fractions.  Designs are selected by certified cost only — never by
their searched worst case — so the instance runs are a test, not a tuning.  The
comparator is the referee's design exactly as published; it is not re-tuned.

## What this cannot show

`BENCHMARK_LIMITATIONS.md`, section BM-1: mean-run model; searched worst cases are lower
bounds; finite design families; one slope, one accuracy.

## Stopping condition

One pass of each job.  Extensions (other slopes, deeper levels, more sizes) are new
ledger entries with their own protocol lines.

## Two predictions added on 2026-11-26 at 21:57 UTC, before any row of M-016 at `n ≥ 16 000`, of M-021, or of M-020 at `n = 16 000` for `u ≥ 1` had been collected

They come from reading the finished rows of M-016 (`n ≤ 8000`) against the theory
division's certified exponents `c_k` of `k` levels (its workstream Z): the overhead of the
best certified design over `n·c_k` grows by 7 to 21 nats per level at `n = 4000` and `8000`, a
fifth level gains 9 and 19 nats in the limit there, and the designs keep `0.22` and nothing
of it.  Rows known when this was written: `certified1` at `n ≤ 8000`; `instances2` at
`n = 4000` (all) and at `n = 16 000` for `u = ½` (part).  Local runs to four levels at
`n = 16 000` and `64 000` (family "theory", coarser search) gave `0.4594` and `0.4125`.

**H13 (the share of the asymptotic gain that is kept).**  At `u = 1`, with either family,
at every size of M-016 from `16 000` to `128 000`: (a) the share kept of a third level's gain
in the limit is at least `0.80`, of a fourth level's between `0.40` and `0.80`; (b) a fifth
level keeps less than half of its gain in the limit (`n a (c_4 − c_5)`, i.e. `38`, `76`, `152`,
`304` nats at `n = 16 000 … 128 000`), and a sixth level less than a quarter of its own.
*Refuted by* one size and level outside these ranges among the rows whose searches
finished; rows that ran into their time limit are reported and do not count.
**H14 (the crossover of the two rules of design is a matter of size in nats).**  In job
M-020 at `n = 16 000`: for `u = ½` (`n a = 485`), `u = 1` (`1775`) and `u = 2` (`5245`) the three-level
design with servers by certified cost has the smaller least worst instance found in
reading (B) — all three sizes are above the few hundred nats at which the rule starts
to certify nested runs.  *Refuted by* one of the three slopes where it has not.
(Disclosure: for `u = ½` the partial rows at `n = 16 000` were already in hand and pointed
that way — `0.5259` against `0.6065` with eleven and three of the twelve fractions — so H14
is a prediction for `u = 1` and `u = 2` only; `u = ½` is reported, not counted.)

Recorded on 2026-11-27, when jobs M-016 and M-021 were closed (`../X-011_ADDENDUM.md`):

  of the 33 stored cells of the two jobs end with an unfinished search, which is why H1 and part
  (b) of H13 are not decided at the larger sizes.

* H12, H13, H1 and H3 were evaluated on the late rows by blocks added to the table generator
  *after* the rows had been collected (`x011_h12`, `x011_h13_check_large`, `x011_h1_large`,
  `x011_h3_large`).  They print the quantity each hypothesis names next to the range written
  above; the column on the rounding of the constants `c_k` is an addition the protocol did not
  foresee.
