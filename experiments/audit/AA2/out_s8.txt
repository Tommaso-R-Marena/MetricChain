AA2 / S8: fibre averages are not u-Lipschitz when the known part has interactions
excess := largest |ln W(a) - ln W(a')| over one outer flip, divided by u, for the member psi(a, b) = gamma |b| of L_u
(gamma = -u or +u), which ignores the outer block; for a product reference the excess is 0.
The member psi(a, b) = gamma |b| + s u |a| (s = +1 or -1, also in L_u) then has a fibre average that changes by
u (1 + excess) per outer flip: fact (F2) of Theorem 11.52, with the constant u, fails as soon as the excess is positive;
with excess <= c it holds with the constant (1 + c) u ('bounded influence'), and where the excess grows with n it
holds with no constant of the order of u.
