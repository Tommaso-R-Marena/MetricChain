## 2. The positive finding (F1): the hypothesis is exact at `B = 2` and only there  [P by the referee; N on 39 families; the three sharpness instances checked by hand by the coordinator]

Your proof discards a factor `B − 1` at l.332: a face with `k` separators holds wholly unpaired blocks, each of
which carries `B − 1` loose bonds, not one.  Keeping it:

> **Theorem 1c′.**  Let `g` satisfy (C0), (H1), (C2) and `|x| < min(B·|σ|, |σ| + γ)`.  Put
> `E*_x = σM + x(n − m) + nΔG_assoc` and `ε_x = min(|σ|, |σ| + γ − |x|, B·|σ| − |x|)`.  Then every connected
> unpseudoknotted structure of `I(a, m, B)` has energy `E*_x` if it is a tiling structure and at least `E*_x + ε_x`
> otherwise.  For every `m ≥ 2` neither the hypothesis nor `ε_x` can be improved.

(For `B = 1` this is your case `|x| < |σ|`; for `B = 2` your hypothesis; for `B ≥ 3` it is weaker.  The proof — four
changes to yours — and the sharpness instances are in the report, §2.2.)  The instances, each checkable by hand:
`a = (2B, B, …, B)` with the long item across the first separator, the `A` bulged: `E − E*_x = |σ| + g_A − |x|`; the
same with the long item on block 2 only: `B|σ| − |x|`; and for `B = 1`, `a = (2)`, `m = 2` (`GAG` + `CC`): four
one-pair structures at `E*_x − (|x| − |σ|)`.

**What it changes.**  For a fixed loop-energy model, your Corollary 2 for coaxial models needs only
`|x| < |σ| + γ`: take instances with `B > |x|/|σ|`, which multiplying all sizes by a constant achieves — as you do
after Theorem 1e.  Your referee's observation "the hypothesis is exact on my grid" is this statement seen from
the side of `B = 2` (its 46 instances contain it).  **What it does not change** — and this is the limitation that
matters physically, which your §9a already states: at the boundary `x = σ` with `γ ≤ 0` (a nicked stack as strong
as a stack, a one-base bulge free or favourable) the condition `|x| < |σ| + γ` fails as before.  F1 widens the
theorem where bulges cost something; it does not reach the parameter sets in use if those have `γ ≤ 0`.
