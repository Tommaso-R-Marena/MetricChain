## 0. Result

Every statement the brief asked for is machine-checked, and nothing in the paper
statements was found false.  **Part A:** the remainder identity of workstream P3's
anchored expansion (its Proposition 1) for every order `m`, its corollary with anchored
constants, the allocation lemma at order `m`, and the sandwich
`I_{m−1}g − Σγ_i ≤ g ≤ I_{m−1}g + Σγ_i` — also in a companion file stated on the theory
division's own definitions, where their Theorem 11.18 (`higherOrder_remainder`) is
re-derived as a corollary of the identity.  **Part B:** the two-point Bennett–Hoeffding
bound that the theory division asked for (THY-RES-0009 §7.4; their Theorem 11.110(f)),
on finite laws and on a general probability space, with its extremal law, the whole
chain `0 ≤ K ≤ W(b, σ²) ≤ ln(1 + (σ²/b²)(e^b − 1 − b)) ≤ (σ²/b²)(e^b − 1 − b)`, the lower
bound under a ceiling, the deterministic certificate, and the certificate with its
probability for `N` independent samples.  **One improvement was found and
machine-checked:** `b ↦ W(b, σ²) − b` is nonincreasing, so the upper end of the
certificate holds with `W(r̄ − η − lo, σ²)` in place of `W(r̄ + η − lo, σ²)`.

## 3. What the formalisation found about the paper statements (none false)

* **F1 (weaker hypothesis).**  The corollary of Proposition 1 needs the constants only
  at base points that equal the anchor from `min T` on (`abs_remainder_le_of_ordered`);
  on random functions the sum of such constants was as low as `0.60` of the anchored sum
  [N].
* **F2 (redundant).**  `c_T ≥ 0` is not needed in the sandwich (it follows from the bound
  at the anchor); in the share form of the allocation lemma `c` may have any sign.
* **F3.**  Proposition 1 holds for `m = 0` and for infinite state spaces; P3 §3.7 needs
  no re-ordering of the sites.
* **F4 (redundant).**  In the two-point bound `E X ≤ 0` suffices (not `= 0`), `X ≤ b` is
  needed on the support only, and `v ≥ 0` is automatic.
* **F5 (an improvement, machine-checked).**  The upper end of the certificate holds with
  `W(r̄ − η − lo, σ²)`.  The gain lies in `[0, 2η]`: `0.2 %` of `2η` at P3's certified
  inputs for the twenty-residue instance and at the theory division's example, `70 %` of
  `2η` for a floor of `8 k_BT` [N].  No literature was searched for it; no novelty is
  claimed.
* **F6.**  At `lo = r̄ + η` the formula `W(0, σ²)` is undefined as written; the statement
  holds with `W(0, ·) = 0` (`bennettW_zero_left`).

## 4. What is not formal

The rest of P3's construction: its Lemma 2 (the Faà di Bruno bound), Theorem 3,
Proposition 4 (the closed-form sum over subsets), Theorem 5 as a statement about the
banded models and their transfer matrices, Proposition 6 (iii); the certified constants
themselves (interval enclosures in floating point).  The bijection of the paper proof of
Proposition 1 is not formalised as a bijection — the identity is proved by induction on
the largest site.  Part B: strict monotonicity of `W`; the limit `b ↓ 0`; the remainder
chain and the certificate on a general probability space (only the two-point bound is
general); the variance bounds of addendum A17 (7)–(8); the theory division's Theorem
11.110(a).  So **what is machine-checked is the logical skeleton of the certified free
energy of SCI-MSG-0020 — identity, allocation, sandwich, bracket, probability — and not
its constants.**
