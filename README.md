# MetricChain — consolidated research archive

This branch (`research/consolidated-archive`) holds one directory, [`consolidated/`](consolidated/): a
snapshot of the work of the eight MetricChain research divisions at pinned commits of their branches,
assembled for independent assessment.  Start with
[`consolidated/00_MASTER_INDEX.md`](consolidated/00_MASTER_INDEX.md).

This is the final version (2026-11-27); the interim version of the same day is commit `5c2bcec` of this
branch.  What changed between them: §0.5 of the master index.

* Everything under `consolidated/01_…` to `08_…` is a byte-for-byte copy of a division's own files;
  provenance and checksums are in `consolidated/MANIFEST.json`.
* All of it is the work of AI sessions, reviewed only by other AI sessions.  Nothing has been read by an
  external expert or compared with an experiment.
* The original branches are unchanged; this branch shares no history with them.

Verify a copy with `python3 consolidated/11_reproducibility/verify_manifest.py`.
